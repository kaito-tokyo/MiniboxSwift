macOS Runner Setup

Complete these steps in the virtual machine. The checklist is a visual aid only and is not saved.

Setup Assistant

Enable Remote Login Manually

After the desktop appears, open Terminal in the guest and run:

sudo systemsetup -setremotelogin on
sudo systemsetup -getremotelogin

If macOS reports that Full Disk Access is required, open System Settings > General > Sharing and turn on Remote Login there.

Automatic Finalization

After Remote Login is enabled, Minibox automatically configures the remaining runner settings through the guest startup service. It verifies them and shuts down macOS from inside the guest.

Do not close MiniboxView or shut down the host-side VM. Wait for the guest to power off automatically.

If automatic finalization fails, the guest remains running. Check /var/log/minibox-finalize.log from the serial console.
